# Strategies for Learning

This is not a typical textbook. Our goal is for you to work through the material on your own time and in the way that is most efficient given your prior knowledge.

While you will have more flexibility than you do in a traditional lecture-only course, you will also have more responsibility for your own learning. You will need to:

- Plan how to work through each unit.
- Determine how to use the various features of the book to help you learn.
- Decide when you need to seek additional support.

## What You Need to Know about Each Unit

Each unit in this book has features designed to support you as an independent learner, including the following:

- **Explanatory content:** The content is the informational "meat" of every section. It consists of short passages of text with information, examples, figures, and explanations.
- **Concept Checks:** These interactive questions give you the chance to practice the concept that you are learning, with hints and feedback to guide you if you struggle. Answer them honestly before revealing the answer—the goal is to find out what you actually understand.
- **Examples and comments:** Worked examples show each idea in the context of real data and real studies; comments flag common points of confusion.

Your instructor will provide guidance and instructions for the statistical technology (software or calculator) used in your section of the course. Wherever this book says "using statistical software or a calculator," follow your instructor's technology instructions.
